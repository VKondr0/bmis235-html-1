THE HUMAN EXCHANGE

The Price of a Normal Day

Some things don't have a price tag, but I still end up trading them. If I stay up to finish an assignment, I lose sleep. If I go out with friends, that's time I could've spent studying.

So I made a fictional stock market for sleep, free time, focus, energy and social life. The numbers aren't real, but the choices behind them are.

  • FOC - Focus 118.40 HX +2.3%
  • ENG - Energy 78.60 HX -3.1%
