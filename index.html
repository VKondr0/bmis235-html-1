THE HUMAN EXCHANGE

The Price of a Normal Day

What if the stock market traded things like sleep, free time and focus instead of companies? That's the idea behind The Human Exchange. The prices aren't real, but the choices are pretty familiar.

College students make these trades all the time. Staying up late to finish an assignment might mean two less hours of sleep. And sometimes a free Friday afternoon feels more valuable than money.

Human Assets

  • SLP - Sleep: down 4.6% during exam week.
  • FOC - Focus: down 3.2% after too many browser tabs.
  • TIM - Free Time: up 1.9% as Friday gets closer.
  • ENG - Energy: down 1.1% after a long week.
  • SOC - Social Life: up 2.7% over the weekend.

All market data is fictional.
